URL: https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2024/09/end-of-month-liquidity-in-the-treasury-market/

Captured claims from Federal Reserve Bank of New York Liberty Street Economics, September 24, 2024:

- Trading activity in benchmark U.S. Treasury securities concentrates on the last trading day of the month.
- Since 2020, benchmark Treasury trading activity was roughly 46 percent higher on the last trading day of the month on average.
- The authors conjecture that turn-of-month portfolio rebalancing by passive funds helps explain the pattern.
- Higher end-of-month volume was associated with lower price impact and improved liquidity rather than higher volatility in that market.
