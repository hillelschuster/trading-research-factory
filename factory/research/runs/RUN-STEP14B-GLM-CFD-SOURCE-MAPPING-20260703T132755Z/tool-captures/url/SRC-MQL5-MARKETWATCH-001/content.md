<H1>Market Watch</H1>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">The Market Watch window provides an overview of price data of financial instruments: quotes, price statistics and tick chart. It also provides details of contract specifications and One Click Trading options.</span></p>
<H2><a id="symbols" class="hmanchor"></a>How to View Quotes of Financial Instruments <a class="anchor" href="#symbols">#</a></H2>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">The Market Watch features real-time quotes of financial instruments and other price data: spread, volume, etc.</span></p>
<div class="screenshotContainer" > <img class="help" src="/i/help/terminal/en/market_watch.png" width="634" height="275" alt="Market Watch">
  <div class="point" title="87;63">
    <p>Financial instrument name</p>
  </div>
  <div class="point" title="206;63">
    <p>Bid price</p>
  </div>
  <div class="point" title="283;63">
    <p>Ask price</p>
  </div>
  <div class="point" title="359;63">
    <p>The last price at which the deal was executed</p>
  </div>
  <div class="point" title="435;63">
    <p>The volume of the last executed deal</p>
  </div>
  <div class="point" title="500;63">
    <p>Spread — the difference between the Bid and Ask prices</p>
  </div>
  <div class="point" title="572;63">
    <p>The last quote arrival time. Specified according to the time zone of the broker's trading server.</p>
  </div>
</div><p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">The displayed data can be configured in the context menu. For example, you can show the &quot;Source&quot; field </span><span class="f_ol">— the provider of the financial instrument liquidity. </span><span class="f_txt">To save the current state of Market Watch data to a CSV file, select 'Export'. All columns and their values displayed at the time of export will be saved to the file. This data can then be analyzed using third-party applications.</span></p>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">Double-clicking on one of the instruments opens <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading/performing_deals#position_open" class="topiclink">a new position opening</a> window. A symbol chart can be opened by dragging it to the chart viewing area using a mouse (Drag'n'Drop); in this case windows of currently open charts will be closed. If you hold down &quot;Ctrl&quot; while dragging, the chart is opened in a separate <a href="/en/terminal/help/startworking/interface" class="topiclink">tab</a>, and other charts remain open.</span></p>
<div ><div class="parent-table"><table class="attentable" cellpadding="8" border="1">
<tr class="attentable">
<td class="attentable"><ul >
<li class="p_tableatten"><span class="f_tableatten">If there are <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading/performing_deals#position_open" class="topiclink">open positions</a> or <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading/performing_deals#pending" class="topiclink">pending orders</a> for the financial instrument, or when its chart is open, the instrument cannot be hidden from the Market Watch.</span></li>
<li class="p_tableatten"><span class="f_tableatten">If a symbol is hidden in Market Watch, its data cannot be used in <a href="/en/terminal/help/algotrading/autotrading" class="topiclink">MQL5 programs</a> and the <a href="/en/terminal/help/algotrading/testing" class="topiclink">Strategy Tester</a>.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul >
<li class="p_tableatten"><span class="f_tableatten">In the High and Low columns of symbols whose charts <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading/market_watch#charts" class="topiclink">are built at Bid prices</a>, the Bid High and Bid Low prices are displayed. If a symbol chart is constructed using Last prices, Last High and Last Low prices are shown for this symbol. If Market Watch contains at least one symbol whose chart is drawn based on Last prices, the Last column is automatically enabled in addition to High/Low.</span></li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
</table></div>
</div>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">Prices in the &quot;Market Watch&quot; window have different colors:</span></p>
<ul >
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Blue</span><span class="f_ol"> — the current price is higher than the previous one;</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Red</span><span class="f_ol"> — the current price is lower than the previous one;</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Gray</span><span class="f_ol"> — the price has not changed for the last 15 seconds.</span></li>
</ul>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">If the <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading/depth_of_market" class="topiclink">depth of market</a> and the Last trade price is available for a symbol, the color is determined by the Last price. Otherwise, the color is determined by the Bid price.</span></p>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">The individual background coloring for symbols can also be configured on the server. This enables their visual distinguishing by types, exchange and other properties. If you do not want to use the specified coloring, enable the &quot;Use system colors&quot; option in the context menu.</span></p>
<H3>How to Quickly Add Symbols</H3>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">To quickly add a symbol in the Market Watch, click </span><span class="f_txt" >+</span><span class="f_txt"> below the list and enter the name of the symbol. While you type in the name, the list of suitable symbols is shown.</span></p>
<p class="p_Image"><img class="help" alt="To quickly add a symbol click on + at the bottom and enter the name" title="To quickly add a symbol click on + at the bottom and enter the name" width="750" height="374"  src="/i/help/terminal/en/market_watch_fast_add.png"/></p>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">Information about the current number of symbols in the Market Watch and the total number of available symbols is available at the bottom of the list.</span></p>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">If you work with different brokers or markets, you can save separate Market Watch settings for each of them. Create a list of symbols, select the desired columns and click &quot;Symbol Sets — Save as&quot;. The saved set becomes available for quick switching through the same menu.</span></p>
<H3><a id="sort" class="hmanchor"></a>How to Sort Symbols <a class="anchor" href="#sort">#</a></H3>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">To sort the list of symbols, click on any column header. For example, the list can be sorted by symbol name, close price, daily change or other variables. The context menu features the most popular sorting options. <li class="p_ol"><a id="calculation" class="hmanchor"></a><span class="f_ol" >Calculation</span><span class="f_ol"> — method used for <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading_advanced/margin_forex" class="topiclink">margin</a> calculation: Forex, Forex No Leverage, Futures, Exchange Futures, Exchange Stocks, FORTS Futures, Collateral.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><a id="charts" class="hmanchor"></a><span class="f_ol" >Chart mode</span><span class="f_ol"> — symbol chart (<a href="/en/terminal/help/trading_advanced/price_data#charts" class="topiclink">bar</a>) construction mode: by last deal prices (Last) or by Bid prices. The first option is usually used for exchange instruments.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Tick size </span><span class="f_ol">— minimum price change step.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Tick value </span><span class="f_ol">— cost of a single price change point.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Trade</span><span class="f_ol"> — symbol trading mode (full access, long only, short only, close only). Also, trading can be completely prohibited.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Execution</span><span class="f_ol"> — <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading/general_concept#execution_type" class="topiclink">execution mode</a>: Instant, Request, Market, Exchange.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Type of orders</span><span class="f_ol"> — types of orders placed:</span><ul >
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >Good till today including SL/TP</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — orders that are valid only during one trading day. With the end of the day, all of the Stop Loss and Take Profit levels, as well as pending orders are deleted.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >Good till canceled</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — pending orders are preserved for the next trading day.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >Good till today excluding SL/TP</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — only pending orders are deleted at the end of a trading day, while Stop Loss and Take Profit levels are preserved.</span></li>
</ul>

<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Filling</span><span class="f_ol"> — available <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading/general_concept#fill_policy" class="topiclink">fill policies</a>: Fill or Kill, Immediate or Cancel, Book or Cancel, Return.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Expiration</span><span class="f_ol"> — available <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading/performing_deals#pending_place" class="topiclink">types of expiration</a> of pending orders:</span><ul >
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >Good till Canceled</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — order lifetime is unlimited.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >Intraday</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — orders are canceled at the end of the current trading.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >Specified time</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — the order is canceled at a user-specified time.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >Date</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — the order is canceled at the end of the day specified by the user.</span></li>
</ul>

<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Orders</span><span class="f_ol_2lev">— allowed <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading/general_concept#order_type" class="topiclink">order types</a>: market, limit, stop, stop-limit, Stop Loss and Take Profit.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Minimal volume</span><span class="f_ol"> — minimal volume of a deal for the symbol.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Maximal volume</span><span class="f_ol"> — maximal volume of a deal for the symbol.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Volume step</span><span class="f_ol"> — volume change step.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Volume limit</span><span class="f_ol"> — maximum allowable total volume of an open position and pending orders at the same symbol and in the same direction (buy or sell). For example, the limit is 5 lots. If you have a buy position of 5 lots, you can place a Sell Limit order of 5 lots. But you cannot place a pending Buy Limit order (since the total volume in one direction will exceed the limit) or place a Sell Limit order above 5 lots.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Swap type</span><span class="f_ol"> — type of swap calculation:</span><ul >
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >In points</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — the specified number of points of the security price.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >In the base currency</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — the specified amount in the symbol base currency.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >In the margin currency</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — the specified amount in the symbol margin currency.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >In the deposit currency</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — the specified amount in the deposit currency.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >As a percentage of current price</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — the specified percentage of the symbol price at the time of swap calculation.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >As a percentage of the open price</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — the specified percentage of the position open price.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >In points, re-open at Close price</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — the position is closed at the end of the trading. The next day the position is re-opened at the close price +/- the specified number of points.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol_2lev" >In points, re-open at the Bid price</span><span class="f_ol_2lev"> — the position is closed at the end of the trading day. The next day the position is re-opened at the Bid price +/- the specified number of points.</span></li>
</ul>

<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Swap long</span><span class="f_ol"> — swap for Buy positions.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Swap short</span><span class="f_ol"> — swap for Sell positions.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Swap rates</span><span class="f_ol"> — swap multiplier for each day of the week. Swap of long and short positions is multiplied by the specified ratio when charging. Example: the basic swap value for long positions is USD 1.5. For all weekdays, the ratio is equal to one, except Wednesday when it is equal to 3. This means the swap of USD 1.5 is charged when rolling over a position from Monday to Tuesday. The same happens when rolling over the position from Tuesday to Wednesday. When the position is rolled over from Wednesday to Thursday, the swap is tripled — USD 4.5 getting back to the subsequent value in subsequent days. The swap is not charged for weekdays with no ratio specified.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >First trade</span><span class="f_ol"> — the day when the financial instrument trading started.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Last trade</span><span class="f_ol"> — the day when the financial instrument trading ended.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Face value</span><span class="f_ol"> — nominal bond value set by the issuer.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Accrued interest</span><span class="f_ol"> — part of the coupon interest of bonds, which is calculated in proportion to the number of days since the coupon bond issue date or since the previous coupon payment.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Category</span><span class="f_ol"> — the property is used for additional marking of financial instruments. For example, this can be the market sector to which the symbol belongs: Agriculture, Oil &amp; Gas and others.</span></li>

<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >CFI</span><span class="f_ol"> — instrument classification in accordance with the <a href="https://www.iso.org/standard/44799.html" target="_blank" class="weblink">ISO 10962</a> standard.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Sector</span><span class="f_ol"> — economic sector the instrument belongs to, such as energy, finance, healthcare and others. </span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><a id="industry" class="hmanchor"></a><span class="f_ol" >Industry</span><span class="f_ol"> — industry branch the instrument belongs to, such as sportswear, accessories, car manufacturing, restaurant business and others. <li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Country</span><span class="f_ol"> — country of the company whose shares are traded on the stock exchange.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Option type</span><span class="f_ol"> — <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading/options_board" class="topiclink">call or put</a>.</span></li>

<H3><a id="margin" class="hmanchor"></a>Margin <a class="anchor" href="#margin">#</a></H3>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">The instrument specification may include the following values related to <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading_advanced/margin_forex" class="topiclink">margin calculations</a>:</span></p>
<ul >
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Initial margin </span><span class="f_ol">— security deposit (margin) provided for a fixed-term contract to perform a one-lot deal. If the initial margin value is specified for the symbol, this is the value that is used. <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading_advanced/margin_forex" class="topiclink">Margin calculation</a> formulas are not applied to the appropriate calculation type.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Maintenance margin </span><span class="f_ol">— minimum security deposit (margin) a trader should have on his or her account to maintain a one-lot position.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol" >Hedged margin</span><span class="f_ol"> — the margin charged per one lot of <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading_advanced/margin_forex#hedged" class="topiclink">hedged</a> positions. You can also select here margin calculation mode for hedged positions — using the larger leg.</span></li>
</ul>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">In addition, the specification contains a detailed table with margin <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading_advanced/margin_forex#rate" class="topiclink">rates</a> and calculated values for each type of trading operation.</span></p>
<p class="p_Image"><img class="help" alt="Information about margin requirements for an instrument" title="Information about margin requirements for an instrument" width="750" height="430"  src="/i/help/terminal/en/specification_margin.png"/></p>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">The type of margin calculation is displayed at the top:</span></p>
<ul >
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol">Notional value — common margin calculation based on the instrument type: <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading_advanced/margin_forex" class="topiclink">Retail Forex, Futures</a> or <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading_advanced/margin_exchange" class="topiclink">Exchange model</a>.</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol">Floating: **** — additional rates can be applied to the common margin calculation depending on the volume or notional value of positions on the account. In this case, the table will indicate the corresponding ranges. This option is only used for the <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading_advanced/margin_forex#rate" class="topiclink">Retail Forex, Futures</a> mode.</span></li>
</ul>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">The &quot;Initial&quot; and &quot;Maintenance&quot; columns display <a href="/en/terminal/help/trading_advanced/margin_forex#rate" class="topiclink">margin rates</a>. They are followed by the margin calculated based on the instrument type and rates.</span></p>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">In the above example, the margin depends on the volume of positions in the account. Three ranges are available:</span></p>
<ul >
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol">0 - 10</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol">10 - 20</span></li>
<li class="p_ol"><span class="f_ol">&gt;=20</span></li>
</ul>
<p class="p_txt"><span class="f_txt">Suppose there is already a position of 8 lots on the account, and you open a new position of 4 lots. In this case, the first two lots of the position will fall into the first range, and the applied margin will be 1,082.53 * 2 = 2,165.06. The other two lots will fall into the second range, and the margin will be 2,165.06 * 2 = 4,330.12. The total margin for the entire position will be equal to 2,165.06 + 4,330.12 = 6,495.18.</span></p>
