This indicator plots essential session opening ranges, allowing traders to identify potential<b> liquidity sweeps, breakout zones, fakeouts,</b> and <b>key intraday levels</b> across the major global Forex sessions.</p> <p>This strategy is rooted in the concept of the <strong>Opening Range,&nbsp;</strong>a predefined time box following each session open that helps traders determine market maker intentions, short term bias, and intraday directional movement. By marking the high and low of the range and extending these levels, the indicator visually assists in monitoring <strong>Smart Money Breakouts</strong>, <strong>session sweeps</strong>, <strong>false breakouts</strong>, <strong>Fair Value Gaps (FVGs)</strong>, and <strong>range expansions</strong>.</p> <p>The tool highlights four major session based timeboxes:</p> <ul> <li> <p><strong>London Opening Range Breakout (07:00–08:00 GMT)</strong>: Often considered the most volatile session, London ORB is essential for capturing the initial <strong>range expansion</strong> and <strong>liquidity grabs</strong> during the London session open. It serves as a base to identify <strong>trapped traders</strong>, <strong>continuation breakouts</strong>, or early <strong>false moves</strong>.</p></li> <li> <p><strong>New York Opening Range Breakout (13:00–14:00 GMT)</strong>: Critical for spotting <strong>intraday reversals</strong>, <strong>breaker blocks</strong>, and <strong>liquidity runs</strong> into the New York session, this range supports traders aiming to align with <strong>institutional order flow</strong>. It offers key insight into <strong>Smart Money reversals</strong> ahead of <strong>London close</strong> and provides structure for afternoon <strong>range consolidations</strong>.</p></li> <li> <p><strong>Tokyo Opening Range Breakout (00:00–01:00 GMT)</strong>: Although frequently ignored, Tokyo ORB plays a vital role in detecting <strong>Asia session liquidity sweeps</strong>, which can later fuel <strong>London breakout setups</strong>. This time range helps identify <strong>support/resistance traps</strong> formed by early volume accumulation.</p></li> <li> <p><strong>Midnight Opening Range (00:00–01:00 GMT)</strong>: Used as a <strong>market maker range anchor</strong>, the Midnight OR defines the start of the daily structure. It supports <strong>daily bias mapping</strong>, <strong>initial liquidity hunts</strong>, and the alignment of <strong>Asia-London crossover setups</strong>.</p></li> </ul> <p>Each session timebox is customizable by Server-based start and end times and is plotted visually as a box on the chart, with extended high/low levels for <strong>sweep detection</strong>. These levels serve as reference points for <strong>breakout strategies</strong>, <strong>entry confirmation</strong>, and <strong>liquidity hunt reaction zones</strong>.<br/><br/></p> <h3><strong>This tool is ideal for:</strong><br/> </h3> <ul> <li> <p>ICT and Smart Money traders who use <strong>session based entry models</strong></p></li> <li> <p>Traders monitoring <strong>institutional liquidity</strong>, <strong>order blocks</strong>, and <strong>fair value gaps</strong></p></li> <li> <p>Intraday Forex traders focused on <strong>London and New York volatility windows</strong></p></li> <li> <p>Traders using <strong>Killzones</strong>, <strong>initial balance</strong>, or <strong>opening range breakouts</strong> as part of their setup</p></li> <li> <p>Those who track <strong>liquidity inducements</strong>, <strong>market structure shifts</strong>, and <strong>bias flips</strong></p></li> </ul> <h3><strong>Key Features:</strong><br/> </h3> <ul> <li> <p>Detects breakouts across London, New York, Tokyo, and Midnight sessions</p></li> <li> <p>Draws real time <strong>session boxes</strong>, <strong>high/low levels</strong>, and <strong>range sweep alerts</strong></p></li> <li> <p>Highlights potential <strong>bullish/bearish breakout zones</strong> based on session dynamics</p></li> <li> <p>Supports detection of <strong>FVGs (Fair Value Gaps)</strong> and displays <strong>gap fill percentage</strong></p></li> <li> <p>Offers full visual customization including color themes, font size, and marker symbols</p></li> <li> <p>Optimized for <strong>low latency intraday analysis</strong></p></li> </ul> <h3><strong><br/>Trading Strategy and Use Case:</strong><br/> </h3> <p>The Opening Range Breakout (ORB) strategy begins with the trader defining the key session timebox, typically the first hour after the market opens. The high and low of this range are used as <strong>liquidity reference points</strong>. The market often consolidates inside this range before executing a <strong>liquidity hunt</strong>, <strong>fake breakout</strong>, or <strong>range continuation</strong>.</p><span><br/><b>What to look for:</b></span><br/> <ul> <li> <p>Monitor for <strong>sweeps of the high or low</strong> followed by a <strong>rejection</strong>, signaling a potential <strong>reversal or entry</strong> in the opposite direction</p></li> <li> <p>Look for <strong>breakouts</strong> with volume confirmation or price displacement for <strong>momentum entries</strong></p></li> <li> <p>Combine breakout levels with <strong>Smart Money concepts</strong> such as <strong>breaker blocks</strong>, <strong>FVGs</strong>, and <strong>order block reactions</strong></p></li> <li> <p>Use the <strong>session range</strong> to gauge <strong>initial balance shifts</strong>, <strong>bias determination</strong>, and <strong>liquidity inducement traps</strong></p></li> </ul> <p><strong>Entry and Exit:</strong></p> <ul> <li> <p>Enter after price breaks the range and confirms direction with a <strong>displacement candle</strong> or <strong>liquidity sweep reaction</strong></p></li> <li> <p>Exit at opposing liquidity zones, <strong>session highs/lows</strong>, or after a <strong>fair value gap</strong> is filled</p></li> <li> <p>Use <strong>stop loss just outside the session range</strong> or based on previous swing highs/lows</p></li> </ul> <h3><strong><br/>Best suited pairs:</strong><br/> </h3> <p>This indicator performs well on <strong>major Forex pairs</strong> such as <b>EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY,</b> and <b>USDCAD</b>.
