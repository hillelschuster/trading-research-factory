### Interpretation

The higher volatility hypothesis is partially validated. While SOL didn't generate MORE trades than ETH (14.1 vs 14.3), it achieved SIGNIFICANTLY better risk-adjusted returns (1.606 vs 0.528 Sharpe). The lower drawdown and higher win rate suggest the strategy works well on SOL's more volatile price action. The key improvement was in the quality of trades rather than quantity.

The strategy appears robust across different crypto assets - both ETH and SOL show positive results with the same logic.
