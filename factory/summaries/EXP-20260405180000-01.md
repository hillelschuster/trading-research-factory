# EXP-20260405180000-01: BNB EMA Trend Gate - Extreme RSI Bounds Test

## Objective
Test even looser RSI bounds (20/80) to further exploit BNB exchange token volatility regime discovered in prior run (1.275 Sharpe with 25/60).

Moving RSI bounds from 25/60 to 20/80 would capture more extreme mean-reversion setups in BNB's high-volatility environment.

## Analysis

1. **Hypothesis Rejected**: The hypothesis that "trend toward extremes continues" was NOT validated. The optimizer consistently rejected 20/80 bounds in favor of 25/60.

2. **Performance Improvement**: Despite not validating the extreme bounds hypothesis, overall OOS Sharpe improved from 1.275 to 1.4205 (+11.4%). This likely comes from the broader exploration of hold_bars (144-168 vs prior fixed 168).

3. **Robustness Confirmed**: 57/57 windows completed successfully with consistent parameter selection (rsi_oversold=25, rsi_overbought=60).

## Lessons Learned

- Extreme RSI bounds (20/80) do not improve on 25/60 for BNB
- Optimizer consistently rejects extreme bounds - confirms prior near-optimal selection
- Sharpe improvement likely from broader hold_bars exploration rather than extreme bounds
